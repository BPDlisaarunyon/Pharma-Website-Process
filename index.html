    Glossary

    Terms that show up in this process and aren't always obvious to people new to pharma work.

    Flats
    Static, non-interactive design comps submitted for review — as opposed to a working build.
    IR
    Internal Review — the round where the internal team reviews work before it goes to the client.
    Manuwires
    Wireframes annotated with near-final copy, used to align structure and content together instead of separately.
    MLR
    Medical, Legal, Regulatory review — the required approval round for pharma promotional material.
    Route to clean
    Incorporating a round of feedback and producing a clean, mark-up-free version.
    2253 / AFD
    FDA Form 2253, submitted to the Office of Prescription Drug Promotion at first dissemination of new promotional material.
    Fair balance / ISI
    The risk information that must accompany promotional claims for a prescription product.
    Hard lock
    A cutoff after which no further content or code changes are allowed before launch.
